Product Description

Engineered for active users and outdoor enthusiasts, the D87 Anti-Wind Air Conduction Earphone delivers clear audio while keeping you safe and aware of your surroundings. Featuring an open-ear design that eliminates ear pressure, it is built with an anti-wind noise-reduction microphone structure to ensure crystal-clear voice calls even in breezy conditions or during high-speed activities like cycling and running. Lightweight and ergonomic, it provides long-lasting comfort with a secure, stable fit.
